# Adventurer Generation

There are two types of adventurers: heroes and random adventurers. 

## Hero

A hero is a fixed, pre-designed superior adventurer with an Exceptional Class which appears in specific stages. They are usually stronger than random adventurers and often serve as the main enemies of the stages. 

Additionally, there is a page in the codex to record the Heroes that the player has taken down. (For codex scene only, not related to battlefield)

\*\* Heroes can break the class rule. They are tailor made.

## Random Adventurer

A random adventurer is a normal adventurer whose properties are generated randomly by some algorithm. 

A random adventurer will be assigned a random class (include the basic attack method), some random skills, and a random LV (and equipment TBC).

These properties combined to determine the Max HP, action speed and attack power of the adventurer units.

## Stage generation Logic

At the beginning of a stage, all the enemies of all waves will be randomly generated. However, the player only knows the adventurer teams of the incoming wave. 

The generation logic should use a seed (maybe by date) so the adventurer combination of a stage over a period of time (maybe daily) is always the same, including classes, skills, LV, etc. 

However, the generation seed does not control the positions the adventurers appear in a wave. It also does not affect the card order. Players should experience some degree of difference even though they play a stage with exactly the same adventurer team combination.

To balance the difficulty of stages, the combat power of each adventurer is calculated to adjust the strength of the adventurer teams. The combat power of an adventurer is calculated by class, skills, and level. 

For the seeded generation logic, it should determine the following steps in order:

1. What is the estimated combat power of each wave?

2. Any Heroes? If yes, in which wave?

3. How many adventurers in each wave?

4. What are the classes of the adventurers?

5. What random skills should be assigned to each adventurer?

6. What are the equipments? (TBC)

7. What are the levels of the adventurers? (Adjust the value to meet the estimated combat power)

The above is the high-level idea of the formula. Details and fine-tune are left for the implementation. It is to remind that the classes combination of each wave should not be too condense on some classes.
