# Boss Monster

On the battlefield, the player controls a boss-level monster to defeat the enemy adventurers. The boss monster should be chosen before the game from a list of player-owned boss monsters. 

## Battling

During `Play Time`, the player can take one of two actions to interact with the battle:

- make a **Cue-Shot** by clicking and dragging from the Boss Monster puck to aim and launch it
- play a **Card** from their hand

## Cue-Shot (Drag to Aim)

In `Play Time` (when all pucks on the table are at rest), the player can click on the Boss Monster puck and drag on the table plane to aim a Cue-Shot opposite the drag direction, spending Energy (1.0 to 9.0 slots in 0.5 steps) to launch the Boss Monster puck and enter `Puck-puck Time!`. See `../puckSystem/dragAndShot.md` for details.

## Play a card

In `Play Time`, the player can play a card from their hand. Select a card and click on the battlefield to play its effect (moving the card to the Casting Area, then to the Discard Pile when its cooldown ends). The player can click the card again to cancel the selection.
