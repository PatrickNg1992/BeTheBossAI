# Battlefield Statistic

To allow player make strategy plays, provide statistics for in the battlefield scene. Create a Statistic Manager. Collect these data at correct timing:
- damage dealt by each unit (add up `Puck-puck time!` and Action Time)
- damage taken by each unit
- Number of move skill, attack skill and active skill taken effect

Store the data and show it by stage waves. Allow the player to check them before leaving the game.

## Statistic Display Window

Show the statistics with a pop up window, *Statistic Display Window*. The window can be opened by click a `Statistic` button. The button is located on the top right corner of the screen when in play(below the resume button). When the game is ended also show the button at the middle of the screen.

In the window, there are two rows of buttons and a bar chart. The first row of buttons choose wave. The second row of buttons choose type of data to check: damage dealt/damage taken/skill activation count, and what units to display: adventurers or monsters(Boss and minions).
