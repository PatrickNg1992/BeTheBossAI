# Unit Information Board

Unit Information Board is a canvas board that shows unit information on the battlefield. 



## General

It appears on the left and right of the screen, corresponding to the two sides of the game. In different game modes, each side shows different units.

On each side, the board looks like a table with 2 columns and multiple rows. The cell is called *Unit Display*. Each Unit Display holds a unit. 

### Unit Display

Each cell shows the unit's image. Below the cell is the unit's HP bar. In the upper-left corner of the cell, a *Reference Mark* lets players match the unit and its corresponding pucks. Usually, the Reference Mark is an image or a Roman numeral. 

The Unit Display for boss monsters shall span 2 horizontal cells, making it look amazing. For the unit display of the player's boss monster, it does not show the HP because the information appears in the upper-left corner of the screen. It only shows the current HP bar and remaining HP bar count of the opponent's boss monster, if any.

If the unit is an enemy adventurer, a *Class Mark* appears in the upper-right corner to tell what class the unit is.

When a player hovers the mouse over a Unit Display, the corresponding puck on the table will be highlighted so they can spot the puck easily. When the mouse stays on the Unit Display for a short period, a small pop-up window named *Unit Detail Display* will be shown to the side.

### Unit Detail Display

The Unit Detail Display shows more detail about a unit. It appears when the player hovers over the Unit Display.

## Single-player game

In a single-player PvE game, the left side shows the player's boss monster and the minions, while the right side shows the enemy adventurers. 



## 2-player vs game

Each side shows one player's boss monster and the minions.
