# 03 — Per-Puck Collision History, `(J / 10) * hitter.POW` Collision Damage, Non-Boss Cushion `Hit Level +1`, and Base Passive Skills (`SPA10001 Protect`, `SPA10003 Safety Rope`)

**What to build:** the skill-driven collision and pocket passive pipeline during `Puck-puck Time!` and `Settle Time`. Each puck records an ordered **Collision History** of pucks and cushions collided during the turn (cleared at the end of `Settle Time`). All default class-based collision rules are removed: by default, Boss Monsters take `0` collision damage from puck impacts, while all non-Boss pucks take collision damage equal to **`(J / 10) * hitter.POW`** (where `J` is the collision Impulse scalar applied to the victim puck and `hitter.POW` is the hitting unit's `POW`). Every non-Boss unit puck gains **`Hit Level +1`** whenever it collides with a Cushion block during `Puck-puck Time!`. Guard's base passive skill `SPA10001 Protect` (`Lv 1..3`) reduces an opponent Boss Monster's Frenzy Power on hit by `Y% = X * (4% + Lv * 1%)` (where `X` is the number of non-defeated non-Guard-tree teammates in the **same Adventurer Team**, including pocketed teammates, offsetting the normal `+10.0%`; doubled to `2 * Y%` if this Guard puck is the very first puck in the Boss Monster's Collision History for that shot, clamped to `>= 50.0%`), and reduces collision damage taken on **both pucks by `50%` (fixed, applied at most once per collision)** when colliding with a same-team ally. Scout's base passive skill `SPA10003 Safety Rope` (`Lv 1..3`) modifies Pocket Area fixed damage (`50%` baseline) by `-(10 + Lv * 3)%` for all units in the **same Adventurer Team** while that Scout unit is **on the cue table** (excluding the falling Scout itself if it falls into a pocket).
