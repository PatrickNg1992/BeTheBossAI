# 02 — Fixed 12-Turn Action Cycle, Time Board Cycle Tag UI & Model (`3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12`), and 12-Turn `Hit Level` Reset

**What to build:** the Fixed 12-Turn Action Cycle on the Time Board and in the battle clock, plus 12-turn `Hit Level` persistence. Every 12 Turns (charging 12 energy slots) is an Action Cycle, with permanent cycle Action Tags always placed at turns **`3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12`** of each repeating 12-turn cycle (even when `0` units act on that turn), and `12`-interval tags highlighted in a special colour. Every non-defeated Adventurer (across all teams in 1P PvE and 2P PvP, including while pocketed) has a fixed `Action Countdown` (`3`, `4`, or `6`) and contributes `+1` to all multiples of its interval (`3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12`). On the Time Board view, each cycle tag displays an arrow (with `12`-interval arrows highlighted) and a circle on top showing the acting unit count for tags on the **upper bar (within 10 turns)** when `count > 0`; when `count == 0` (or on the lower bar), the circle and number disappear, and no turns-to-goal integer is shown on the Time Board tag itself (each unit's personal `Action Countdown` is shown on its `UnitDisplay`). When a cycle tag reaches the goal with `count == 0`, it clears immediately without entering `Action Time`; when `count > 0`, `Action Time` triggers. At the end of `Settle Time`, acted units reset their personal `Action Countdown` to their fixed interval, defeated units are removed from future cycle tag counts, and `Hit Level` persists across turns `1..11` and resets to `0` **only after the `Settle Time` of Turn 12**. Pause early for human UI verification of the updated Time Board cycle tags.
