# 04 — `AutoFightManager`, `PresentationManager`, Three-Phase `SPD`-Ordered Action Time, `SPA10004 Chanting`, and Base Attack Skills (`SAA10001..10004`)

**What to build:** the battle-scoped **`AutoFightManager`** (controlling the automated flow in `Action Time` and `Settle Time` for all units on the battlefield) and **`PresentationManager`** (controlling visual/sound effects and animations across `Action Time` and `Settle Time`), plus `SPA10004 Chanting` and the four Rank-1 Base Attack Skills (`SAA10001 Lance`, `SAA10002 Melee`, `SAA10003 Shoot`, `SAA10004 Spell Attack`), replacing the legacy continuous `_physics_process` enemy AI and removing the temporary `Action` button and 5-second `Action Time` placeholder timer. When `Action Time` begins, `AutoFightManager` gathers all acting units (whose `Action Countdown` reached `0`, across both Single-Player PvE and 2-Player Versus teams), triggers start-of-action passives via `UnitSkillManager` (`ON_ACTION_TIME_START`, where on-table acting Acolytes increment their `SPA10004 Chanting` counter `N` by `+1` and recalculate `POW` with `+N * (7.5 + Lv * 2.5)%`, skipping the increment for units returning from a Pocket Area), and executes on-table acting units across three sequential skill phases — **all Active Skills first, then all Attack Skills, then all Move Skills**. Within each phase, acting units are ordered by their **`SPD` stat descending** (tie-broken by their order in the game). Skills with a `Skill Countdown` (`9` on `Lance`, `12` on `Spell Attack`) start at their full countdown value at Wave start, tick down by `1` each Turn (even while pocketed, clamping at `0`), and reset to their full countdown value **only when their conditions are met and they activate**. In the Attack Skill phase, triggered attacks play via `PresentationManager` in `SPD` order with a testing cadence of `~0.30s` stagger and `~0.70s` animation duration, trigger a skill popup on the acting unit's `UnitDisplay`, push `unit.POW * skill_ratio` damage onto the target's Damage Stack, and increase the target's `Hit Level` by `Hit Level+X` before `Settle Time`. Units whose triggered Active or Attack Skill has `Skip Movement` (`Lance`, `Melee`, `Spell Attack`) skip the Move Skill phase, whereas Scout's `Shoot` checks range (`7.5` centre-to-centre) and fires from its starting position in the Attack Skill phase and then proceeds to the Move Skill phase.
