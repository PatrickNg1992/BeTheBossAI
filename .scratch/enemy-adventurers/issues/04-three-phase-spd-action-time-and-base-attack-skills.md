# 04 — Three-Phase `SPD`-Ordered Action Time Executor, Skill Countdowns, and Base Attack Skills (`SAA10001..10004`)

**What to build:** the `Action Time` phase executor and the four Rank-1 Base Attack Skills (`SAA10001 Lance`, `SAA10002 Melee`, `SAA10003 Shoot`, `SAA10004 Spell Attack`), replacing the legacy continuous `_physics_process` enemy AI and removing the temporary `Action` button and 5-second `Action Time` placeholder timer. When `Action Time` begins, all on-table acting units (whose `Action Countdown` reached `0`, across both Single-Player PvE and 2-Player Versus teams) execute across three sequential skill phases — **all Active Skills first, then all Attack Skills, then all Move Skills** — rather than one unit executing all of its skills before the next unit. Within each phase, acting units are ordered by their **`SPD` stat descending** (tie-broken by their order in the game). Skills with a `Skill Countdown` (`9` on `Lance`, `12` on `Spell Attack`) start at their full countdown value at Wave start, tick down by `1` each Turn (even while pocketed, clamping at `0`), and reset to their full countdown value **only when their conditions are met and they activate**. In the Attack Skill phase, triggered attacks play in `SPD` order with a testing cadence of `~0.30s` stagger and `~0.70s` animation duration, trigger a skill popup on the acting unit's `UnitDisplay`, push `unit.POW * skill_ratio` damage onto the target's Damage Stack, and increase the target's `Hit Level` by `Hit Level+X` before `Settle Time`. Units whose triggered Active or Attack Skill has `Skip Movement` (`Lance`, `Melee`, `Spell Attack`) skip the Move Skill phase, whereas Scout's `Shoot` checks range (`7.5` centre-to-centre) and fires from its starting position in the Attack Skill phase and then proceeds to the Move Skill phase.
