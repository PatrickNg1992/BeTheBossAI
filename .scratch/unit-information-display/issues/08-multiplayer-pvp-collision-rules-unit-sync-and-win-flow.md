# 08 — Multiplayer PvP Collision Rules, Unit Information Sync & Victory Flow

**What to build:** Implement the 2-Player Versus collision and Frenzy Power rules in `PuckVelocityManager` and `UnitInformationManager` (`1/4` friendly-fire damage with no Frenzy change and `100%` settle multiplier; full opponent damage from either Boss Monster regardless of who triggered `Puck-puck Time!`; `0`-damage Boss-vs-Boss collision with `* 20%` post-collision velocity damping on both Bosses and `+3` Energy to the opponent at most once per `Puck-puck Time!`; same-team-only Knight protection), synchronize all **Unit Information** (`sync_boss_stats`, `sync_units`, `sync_effect_trigger`, `sync_all`) from the Host to all Peers, and trigger the Multiplayer-only `"Player A/B win"` message for 5 seconds before returning all Peers to the Waiting Room once all 6 of an opponent's Adventurers are defeated.

**Blocked by:** 07 — Multiplayer 2-Player Versus Setup: Skeleton Boss, Snooker Triangles & Player B Drag-and-Aim

**Status:** completed

- [x] **Boss Monster vs. Friendly Adventurer**: deals `1/4` (`0.25x`) collision damage, skips any Frenzy Power increase/decrease, and settles with `100%` baseline Frenzy Power (friendly source never applies Frenzy bonus); a friendly Knight does not deal collision damage to its own Boss Monster.
- [x] **Boss Monster vs. Opponent Adventurer**: deals full (`1.0x`) collision damage attributed to the hitting Boss Monster (even when Player B's Boss Monster hits Player A's Adventurers during a `Puck-puck Time!` triggered by Player A), updates the hitting Boss Monster's **Frenzy Power** (`+10%` vs non-Knight, `-20%` vs Knight), and an opponent Knight deals collision damage back to the Boss Monster.
- [x] **Boss Monster vs. Opponent Boss Monster**: deals `0` damage to both Boss Monsters, immediately multiplies both Boss pucks' post-collision velocities by `20%` (`velocity * 0.2`), and grants `+3` Energy to the non-active opponent at most once per `Puck-puck Time!`.
- [x] **Adventurer vs. Adventurer**: Knight collision protection (`0` damage) applies only when both Adventurers belong to the same team.
- [x] **Host → All Peers Unit Information Synchronization**: The Host broadcasts `UnitInformationManager` state (`current_hp`, `max_hp`, `bars_remaining`, `frenzy_power`, `hit_level`, `damage_stack` count & preview Ghost HP, `is_pocketed`, `is_dead`, plus one-shot collision shake and settled `-567` HP change triggers) via `sync_boss_stats()`, `sync_units()`, `sync_effect_trigger()`, and `sync_all()`, so Player A, Player B, and Spectators see identical Unit Information Boards in real time.
- [x] **Multiplayer Victory Flow**: In Multiplayer mode (`has_player_b() == true`), after `Settle Time` settles damage (and any puck defeated by collision or Pocket Area 50% max-HP damage is removed without respawning), if all 6 Adventurers of one player are defeated (`is_dead == true`), the other player wins (if both teams' last Adventurers are defeated in the exact same `Settle Time`, the player who took the shot wins); the Host syncs and displays `"Player A win"` or `"Player B win"` along with `"return in ? second"` counting down across all Peers for **5 seconds**, then ends the battle so all Peers return to the Waiting Room.
- [x] **Multiplayer UX & Pause Sync**: Clicking Pause synchronizes pause state and displays a centered `"Paused"` message across all Peers; when the opponent claims the Playing Right, `"Opponent takes the play!"` flashes on the banner; returning to `Charging Time` highlights the Play button; and clicking an opponent puck during `Play Time` highlights the player's own Boss Monster puck for 1 second.
- [x] Automated multi-peer tests (Host, Player B, Spectator over `FakeTransport`) verify all PvP collision rules, live Unit Information Board synchronization across Peers, and the `"Player A/B win"` 5-second victory transition back to the Waiting Room.
