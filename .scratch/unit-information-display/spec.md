# Spec: Unit Information Display

Status: ready-for-agent

The **Unit Information Board** (with its **Unit Display** cells and **Unit Detail Display** popup), **Reference Marks**, **Unit Information Manager**, and the **Damage Stack / Hit Level / Frenzy Power** damage calculation pipeline. Vocabulary per `CONTEXT.md` and `.docs/contexts/battlefield.md` (Boss Monster, Enemy Adventurer, Adventurer Team, Class, Minion, Health Bar, Puck, Cue Table, Pocket Area, Turn, Time Board, Action Tag, Prepare Time, Charging Time, Play Time, Action Time, Puck-puck Time!, Settle Time, Impulse, Collision Damage, Focusing, Interrupt, Unit Information Board, Unit Display, Unit Detail Display, Reference Mark, Unit Information Manager, Damage Stack, Frenzy Power, Hit Level); settled in the grilling session; raw sources `.docmanual/battleField/unitInformation/unitInformationBoard.md`, `unitInformationManager.md`, and `damageCalculation.md`.

## Problem Statement

On the battlefield today, unit status is fragmented and temporary: enemy health bars float over the 3D Cue Table via screen projection (cluttering the table and hiding during `Play Time` and `Puck-puck Time!`), simple placeholder side cards are dumped into a vertical list with no connection to pucks on the table, and damage is applied directly or through a flat integer dictionary with no distinction between hit count, exponential Hit Level scaling, or Boss Monster Frenzy Power combos. Players cannot match a HUD card to a specific puck on the table, cannot see how many hits a unit has accumulated during `Puck-puck Time!`, cannot see the Ghost HP trail before `Settle Time`, cannot inspect a unit's full stats and skills, and have no structured board layout that scales across Single-Player PvE, 2-Player Co-op, and 2-Player Versus modes.

## Solution

A battle-scoped **Unit Information Manager** paired with a **Unit Information Board** on the left and right edges of the battlefield canvas, completely replacing the floating 2D health bars on the 3D Cue Table:

1. **Two 2×8 Grids (`{L|R}{Row 1..8}{Col 1..2}`)**: Each side of the canvas shows an 8-row, 2-column board of **Unit Displays** (`L11, L12, L21, L22, …, L81, L82` and `R11, R12, …, R82`). Boss Monsters and Adventurer Team headers span 2 horizontal cells (`L1`, `L5`, `R1`, `R5`). Layout adapts cleanly to Single-Player PvE, 2-Player Co-op, and 2-Player Versus modes.
2. **Reference Marks, Class Colour Coding & Puck Stripes**: Every unit has a **Reference Mark** (`Ω` / `ω1..ω6` in Gold for Player A; `Ψ` / `ψ1..ψ6` in Silver for Player B; capital letters `V, B, R, S, F, E` in Deep Grey for Adventurer Team 1; small letters `v, b, r, s, f, e` in Deep Brown for Adventurer Team 2 — standing for **V**anguard, **B**rawler, **R**anger, **S**pell, **F**ree, and **E**xtra) rendered on both the upper-left corner of its Unit Display and the center of all `0..N` active pucks belonging to that unit. Both the puck and the Unit Display border are colour-coded by **Class** (**Knight = Blue**, **Warrior = Red**, **Ranger = Green**, **Mage = Purple**; Minions use a deeper shade of `1..6`; **Boss Monsters = Black**). To distinguish pucks of the same Class across teams and free positions, Team 2 pucks display a 2-sided **White** line stripe (Team 1 has no team stripe), while free position `5` (`F`/`f`) displays a 4-sided **White** X stripe and position `6` (`E`/`e`) displays a 4-sided **Black** X stripe. Unoccupied class slots in random Adventurer Teams remain empty at their fixed grid positions rather than compacting.
3. **Rich Unit Display & Click-to-Open Unit Detail Display**: Each Unit Display shows the unit's portrait, Reference Mark (upper-left), Class Mark (upper-right, for Enemy Adventurers), Action Tag turn countdown(s) (right/lower-right of the portrait, or pocket return turn countdown when pocketed), HP bar with Ghost HP trail along the bottom, and the **Damage Stack** hit-count circle badge on the left of the HP bar (invisible at `0`, shaking on hit). Hovering a Unit Display highlights all active pucks belonging to that unit on the Cue Table; clicking a Unit Display opens the **Unit Detail Display** popup (and clicking elsewhere or clicking the same cell closes it).
4. **Damage Stack, Hit Level & Frenzy Power Pipeline**: During `Puck-puck Time!` and `Action Time`, every damage event pushes a `Damage DO` (`source`, `value`, `damage_type`) onto the target unit's **Damage Stack**. The pre-settle Ghost HP trail reflects $(\sum \text{Damage Stack}) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$ (excluding Frenzy Power, and clamping at `0` of the active Health Bar). At `Settle Time`, Frenzy Power visually spreads to opposing Adventurers and damage settles:
   - **Enemy Adventurer**: $\left(\sum_{i} \text{Damage}_i \times \text{Frenzy}(\text{Source}_i)\right) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$
   - **Boss Monster / Minion**: $(\sum \text{Damage Stack}) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$, with every Boss Monster capped at losing at most **1 Health Bar** per `Settle Time`.
   After settlement, the Damage Stack clears, every unit's `Hit Level` resets to `0`, and each Boss Monster's `Frenzy Power` decays via `new_frenzy = (current_frenzy / 2) + 50%`.

## User Stories

### Board Layout & Game Modes

1. As a player, I want a Unit Information Board on the left and right sides of the battlefield screen, each arranged as an 8-row by 2-column grid (`{L|R}{Row 1..8}{Col 1..2}` in row-major order `L11, L12, L21, L22, …`), so that I can read up to 16 units per side in a consistent spatial layout.
2. As a player, I want the floating 2D health bars above enemy pucks on the 3D Cue Table removed and replaced by the Unit Information Board and Reference Marks, so that the 3D table stays clean and uncluttered.
3. As a player in a Single-Player PvE game, I want Row `L1` (`L11–L12`) to span 2 horizontal cells for my Boss Monster, Rows `L2–L4` (`L21..L42`) to hold up to 6 Minions (`ω1..ω6`), and Rows `L5–L8` left empty, so that my forces are clearly organized on the left board.
4. As a player in a Single-Player PvE game, I want Row `R1` (`R11–R12`) and Row `R5` (`R51–R52`) on the right board reserved as 2-cell team headers showing the Adventurer Team names, with Rows `R2–R4` (`R21..R42`) showing Adventurer Team 1 (`A1..A6`) and Rows `R6–R8` (`R61..R82`) showing Adventurer Team 2 (`B1..B6`), so that multi-team waves are grouped clearly.
5. As a player in a 2-Player Co-op game, I want the left board to show Player A's Boss Monster in `L1` and Minions in `L2–L4`, and Player B's Boss Monster in `L5` and Minions in `L6–L8`, while the right board shows the two Enemy Adventurer Teams as in Single-Player, so that both allies and all enemies fit on the boards.
6. As a player in a 2-Player Versus game, I want the left board to show Player A (`L1` Boss Monster, `L2–L4` Minions, `L5` empty, `L6–L8` 6 Target Pucks) and the right board to show Player B (`R1` Boss Monster, `R2–R4` Minions, `R5` empty, `R6–R8` 6 Target Pucks), so that both competing players have symmetric boards.

### Reference Marks, Class Slot Mapping & Colour Coding

7. As a player, I want every unit's Reference Mark displayed in the upper-left corner of its Unit Display and in the center of all of its pucks on the Cue Table, so that I can immediately match any puck on the table to its Unit Display.
8. As a player, I want Player A's Boss Monster marked as `Ω` and Minions as `ω1..ω6` in **Gold**, and Player B's Boss Monster marked as `Ψ` and Minions as `ψ1..ψ6` in **Silver**, so that player-side pucks and cells are immediately distinguishable.
9. As a player, I want Adventurer Team 1 members marked with capital letters `V, B, R, S, F, E` in **Deep Grey** and Adventurer Team 2 members marked with small letters `v, b, r, s, f, e` in **Deep Brown** (standing for Vanguard, Brawler, Ranger, Spell, Free, and Extra), so that I can tell the two teams and slot roles apart on the table at a glance.
10. As a player facing a normally generated random Adventurer Team, I want position `1` (`V`/`v`) assigned to Knight, `2` (`B`/`b`) to Warrior, `3` (`R`/`r`) to Ranger, `4` (`S`/`s`) to Mage, and `5` (`F`/`f`) and `6` (`E`/`e`) reserved as free positions for duplicate classes, so that a unit's slot letter tells me its role immediately.
11. As a player facing a small or partial Adventurer Team where a class is missing (e.g. no Warrior `B`/`b`), I want that missing position skipped and its corresponding grid cell (`R22` or `R62`) left empty rather than compacting later units into earlier cells, so that every grid position always corresponds to the same slot letter.
12. As a stage designer, I want customized stage Adventurer Teams to be able to override the default class-to-slot restriction when needed, while normal random generation strictly follows `1..4` (`V, B, R, S`) class mapping plus `5..6` (`F, E`) duplicates.
13. As a player, I want both the puck colour and the Unit Display grid border colour to match the unit's **Class** — **Knight = Blue**, **Warrior = Red**, **Ranger = Green**, **Mage = Purple**, **Boss Monster = Black**, and Minions a deeper shade of `1..6` — and I want pucks to carry thin **stripes** (a 2-sided White line stripe on Team 2 pucks vs. none on Team 1; a 4-sided White X stripe on slot `5` `F`/`f` and a 4-sided Black X stripe on slot `6` `E`/`e`), so that I always recognize a Knight by its Blue colour while distinguishing Team 1, Team 2, and Free/Extra pucks at a glance.

### Unit Display Cell & Boss Monster 2-Cell Span

14. As a player, I want each Unit Display to show the unit's image, its Reference Mark in the upper-left corner, its Class Mark in the upper-right corner (for Enemy Adventurers), its Action Tag turn countdown integer(s) on the right/lower-right of the portrait, its HP bar with Ghost HP trail below the cell, and its Damage Stack count circle badge on the left of the HP bar, so that all tactical info fits cleanly in one cell.
15. As a player, I want a Boss Monster's Unit Display to span 2 horizontal cells and display that Boss Monster's current **Frenzy Power** percentage on the right side of the 2-cell display, so that each Boss Monster's combo multiplier is always visible (including when two Boss Monsters are present in 2-player modes).
16. As a seated player (or Spectator), I want the Boss Monster currently displayed in the large upper-left screen HUD (the local seated player's own Boss Monster: Player A's Boss for Player A and Spectators; Player B's Boss for Player B) to omit the HP bar inside its 2-cell Unit Display, while every other Boss Monster on the board (co-op partner's Boss in `L5` or versus opponent's Boss in `L1`/`R1`) shows its current HP bar and remaining Health Bar count (`x N`) inside its 2-cell Unit Display, so that my own HP is not duplicated while other Bosses' HP is readable on the board.
17. As a player, I want a defeated unit's Unit Display to turn grey with a red cross mark over its portrait and hide its HP bar, Damage Stack count badge, and Action Tag turn numbers (while remaining in its grid cell until the Wave ends), so that I instantly know it is out of the battle.
18. As a player, I want a temporarily removed (pocketed) unit's Unit Display to turn grey while keeping its HP bar visible and showing its pocket return turn countdown (`5..1`) in place of normal Action Tag turn numbers, so that I know it is still alive and when it will return.

### Multi-Puck Units, Hover Highlight & Unit Detail Display

19. As a player, I want a single unit (`Unit Information` DO) to support `0..N` active pucks on the Cue Table (0 when pocketed/hidden, 1 normally, or multiple when split/cloned by a skill) while still occupying a single Unit Display cell, so that puck-spawning skills never break the board layout.
20. As a player, when I hover my mouse over a Unit Display, I want all active pucks belonging to that unit highlighted on the Cue Table, so that I can immediately locate that unit's puck(s).
21. As a player, when I click on a Unit Display, I want the **Unit Detail Display** popup to open alongside the board, and when I click elsewhere (or click the same Unit Display again), I want it to close, so that I can inspect a unit without hover flicker.
22. As a player inspecting the Unit Detail Display, I want to see the unit's Name, detailed Class name and Class Mark icon (if an Adventurer), an HP bar displaying the numeric `HP / Max HP` value (plus remaining Health Bar count if a Boss Monster), Attack Power, Puck Mass and Radius, Skills, Special Condition (Focusing, Pocketed + return turns, Stunned/Recovering), Buffs/Debuffs/Statuses, Hit Level, and Frenzy Power (if a Boss Monster), with all labels localized via the multi-language holder, so that I can verify every stat and status effect in detail.

### Damage Stack, Hit Level, Frenzy Power & Visual Effects

23. As a player, whenever a unit takes damage from collisions, effects, or projectiles during `Puck-puck Time!` or `Action Time`, I want a `Damage DO` (storing `source`, `value`, and `damage_type`) appended in time order to that unit's **Damage Stack**, so that no damage is applied prematurely before `Settle Time`.
24. As a player, I want the small circle badge on the left of a unit's HP bar to remain invisible when its Damage Stack count is `0`, appear with the hit count when `> 0`, and shake briefly whenever a new hit is added (with harder hits producing a larger shake), so that multi-hit combos feel impactful.
25. As a player, during `Puck-puck Time!`, when a unit's puck collides on the table, I want its Unit Display to shake proportionally to the **Impulse** received, and when a unit's skill triggers, I want the skill icon to pop up briefly on its Unit Display, so that table impacts and skill activations are reflected on the HUD.
26. As a player, whenever a Boss Monster puck collides with a non-Knight Enemy Adventurer puck, I want that Boss Monster's **Frenzy Power** (baseline `100%`) to increase by `+10%` (default), and whenever it collides with a Knight puck, I want its Frenzy Power to decrease by `-20%` (default) down to a minimum floor of `50%`, so that hitting frail/focusing enemies builds combo damage while hitting Knights punishes my multiplier.
27. As a player, I want every unit (Boss Monsters, Minions, and Enemy Adventurers) to hold an integer **Hit Level** (default `0`, increased or decreased by conditional effects such as hitting a Focusing puck, special cushions, or type effectiveness) that scales final damage exponentially by $1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$, so that conditional setups amplify damage.
28. As a player, during `Puck-puck Time!` and `Action Time`, I want a unit's Ghost HP bar (HP trailing) on its Unit Display to preview pending damage using only its **Damage Stack** and **Hit Level** — $(\sum \text{Damage Stack}) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$ — without including Frenzy Power yet, so that I see the raw staged damage during the action and feel the Frenzy Power multiplier spread as a distinct reward at `Settle Time`.
29. As a player, when `Settle Time` begins after all damage effects complete, I want a visual effect to spread the Boss Monster's **Frenzy Power** to opposing Adventurers' HP bars and settle every unit's Damage Stack into actual damage:
    - For an **Enemy Adventurer**: $\left(\sum_{i \in \text{Damage Stack}} \text{Damage}_i \times \text{Frenzy}(\text{Source}_i)\right) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$, where each `Damage DO` uses the Frenzy Power of the Boss Monster that owns its `source` (`100%` if no Boss Monster source applies).
    - For a **Boss Monster or Minion**: $(\sum \text{Damage Stack}) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$.
30. As a player, I want every Boss Monster's damage in a single `Settle Time` capped at clearing at most **1 Health Bar** — its Ghost HP trail clamps at `0` of the active Health Bar and any excess damage is discarded when the bar breaks and refills the next bar to `100%` — with no exceptions across all Boss Monsters and all Settle Phases, so that a Boss Monster can never be one-shot across multiple bars in a single turn.
31. As a player, immediately after Damage Settlement in `Settle Time`, I want every unit's Damage Stack cleared, every unit's `Hit Level` reset to `0`, each Boss Monster's `Frenzy Power` decayed to `(current_frenzy / 2) + 50%` (pulling values above or below `100%` halfway back toward `100%`), and defeated units checked and transitioned to their defeated display state, so that the next turn begins cleanly.
32. As a developer, I want the **Unit Information Manager** to be a battle-scoped node inside the Battlefield Scene (in group `"unit_information_manager"`) rather than a global project Autoload, so that multiple Battlefield instances in multi-peer tests and scene reloads never collide.
33. As a developer, I want `current_hp`, `max_hp`, `bars_remaining`, and combat stats moved out of the puck physics scripts (`BasePuck`, `BaseBossMonster`, `EnemyAdventurer`) into `UnitInformationDO` inside `UnitInformationManager` (leaving puck scripts with only physics properties: `radius`, `mass`, `rotation`, `position`, `velocity`, `restitution`), and I want Enemy Adventurer default HP scaled up to the thousands level (**Mage = 1500**, **Warrior = 2200**, **Ranger = 2200**, **Knight = 3000**), so that puck physics and unit combat status are cleanly separated and health pools match the multi-hit collision design.
34. As a player, whenever two pucks collide in `PuckVelocityManager` during `Puck-puck Time!`, I want `PuckVelocityManager` to generate a `DamageDO` whose damage value scales linearly with the collision Impulse scalar `J` (landing around `50 ~ 200` damage per collision based on hitting velocity), apply the collision matrix (Enemy Adventurers take collision damage from any impact except with an allied Knight; Boss Monsters only take collision damage from impacts with a Knight), update the Boss Monster's Frenzy Power (`+10%` vs non-Knight, `-20%` vs Knight), and push the `DamageDO` onto the target unit's Damage Stack in `UnitInformationManager`, so that real physics collisions drive the Damage Stack, Ghost HP trail, and Unit Display shake end-to-end.
35. As a player, when my Boss Monster or my Minions take damage during `Puck-puck Time!` or `Action Time` (e.g. Boss Monster colliding with a Knight, Minions colliding or taking enemy attacks, or falling into a Pocket Area), I want their **Damage Stack** hit-count circle badge, blood-red **Ghost HP** trail, gradual green-to-greenish-yellow HP bar, and large settled negative HP change number (`-567`) displayed consistently across both the upper-left Boss Monster HUD (for the local seated player's Boss Monster) and the **Unit Displays** for all other Boss Monsters (`L5`/`R1`) and Minions (`L2–L4`, `L6–L8`, `R2–R4`), so that player-side units and enemy units share the exact same Damage Stack feedback.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Battle-Scoped `UnitInformationManager` (Model / Authority)**:
  - Lives inside the Battlefield Scene (registered in group `"unit_information_manager"`, sibling to `TimingManager`). Not a global Autoload, preserving multi-instance test isolation.
  - Holds an ordered registry of `UnitInformationDO` instances for all units in the battle (alive, pocketed, or defeated) and maps each unit to its assigned board coordinate (`side: "L" | "R"`, `row: 1..8`, `col: 1..2`, `span_cols: 1 | 2`).
  - Emits signals when units are registered/ cleared for a Wave, when a unit's `Damage Stack`, `Hit Level`, `Frenzy Power`, HP/bars, Focusing/Pocketed/Defeated state, Action Tag turn countdowns, or active puck list changes, and when collision impulse / skill trigger / Frenzy spread visual events occur.
- **Data Objects (`UnitInformationDO` and `DamageDO`)**:
  - `DamageDO`: pure data object holding `source_unit_id: Variant`, `owner_boss_unit_id: Variant` (identifies which Boss Monster's Frenzy Power applies, or empty/null for `1.0`), `value: int`, and `damage_type: String`.
  - `UnitInformationDO`: holds identity & board placement (`unit_id`, `unit_kind: "boss" | "minion" | "enemy_adventurer" | "target_puck"`, `owner_seat: "a" | "b" | "enemy"`, `team_index: 1 | 2`, `slot_index: 1..6`, `grid_id: String`, `reference_mark: String`, `reference_mark_color: Color`, `slot_color: Color`, `name_key: String`), combat stats (`adventurer_class`, `current_hp: int`, `max_hp: int`, `bars_remaining: int`, `max_bars: int`, `power: int`, `mass: float`, `radius: float`), dynamic combat state (`damage_stack: Array[DamageDO]`, `hit_level: int`, `frenzy_power: float`, `is_focusing: bool`, `is_pocketed: bool`, `pocket_return_turns: int`, `is_dead: bool`, `action_tag_turns: Array[int]`, `skills: Array`, `statuses: Array`), and `pucks: Array[BasePuck]` (`0..N` active pucks on the Cue Table).
  - **Strict Separation of Physics vs. Unit Stats**: `current_hp`, `max_hp`, `bars_remaining`, `attack_damage`, and damage/death handling are migrated out of the puck scripts (`BasePuck`, `BaseBossMonster`, `EnemyAdventurer`) into `UnitInformationDO` inside `UnitInformationManager`. Puck scripts retain only puck physics properties (`mass`, `radius`, `restitution`, `position`, `velocity`, `rotation`, pocket-fall physics) and a reference/ID to their owning unit; adding or removing a puck notifies `UnitInformationManager.register_puck(unit_id, puck)` / `unregister_puck(unit_id, puck)`.
  - **Thousands-Scale Adventurer HP**: Enemy Adventurer default max HP values are set to **Knight = 3000**, **Warrior = 2200**, **Ranger = 2200**, and **Mage = 1500**.
- **Collision Damage Generation in `PuckVelocityManager`**:
  - Inside `PuckVelocityManager.resolve_puck_puck_collision(p1, p2)`, after computing the collision Impulse scalar `J`, `PuckVelocityManager` invokes collision effect and damage hooks on `UnitInformationManager`:
    - **Linear Impulse-to-Damage Demo Formula**: Collision damage is calculated linearly from the collision Impulse scalar `J` (`damage = round(J * COLLISION_DAMAGE_PER_IMPULSE)`, tuned so typical collisions deal approximately `50 ~ 200` damage based on hitting velocity).
    - **Collision Matrix & Frenzy Power Hook**:
      - Enemy Adventurers suffer collision damage from any impact above the speed threshold, *except* impacts with an allied Knight puck (Knight protection).
      - Boss Monsters only suffer collision damage when colliding with a Knight puck.
      - When a Boss Monster puck collides with a non-Knight Enemy Adventurer puck, that Boss Monster's `Frenzy Power` increases by `+10%`; when colliding with a Knight puck, its `Frenzy Power` decreases by `-20%` (clamped to `>= 50%`).
      - Valid collision damage creates a `DamageDO` (recording the source unit, owning Boss Monster for Frenzy Power attribution, linear impulse damage value, and `"collision"` damage type) and pushes it to the target unit's `Damage Stack` in `UnitInformationManager`, while also triggering the Impulse-proportional `UnitDisplay` shake and Damage Stack badge shake.
- **Reference Mark, Slot Assignment & Colour Rules**:
  - **Coordinates**: `{Side: L|R}{Row: 1..8}{Col: 1..2}` (row-major: Row `r` has `{Side}{r}1` and `{Side}{r}2`; a 2-column horizontal span at Row `r` occupies `{Side}{r}1..{Side}{r}2`, referred to as `Row {Side}{r}`).
  - **Game Mode Layouts**:
    - *Single-Player PvE*: Left `L1` = Player A Boss (`Ω`), `L2–L4` (`L21..L42`) = Player A Minions (`ω1..ω6`), `L5–L8` empty. Right `R1` = Team 1 Header span, `R2–R4` (`R21..R42`) = Team 1 Adventurers (`A1..A6`), `R5` = Team 2 Header span, `R6–R8` (`R61..R82`) = Team 2 Adventurers (`B1..B6`).
    - *2-Player Co-op*: Left `L1` = Player A Boss (`Ω`), `L2–L4` = Player A Minions (`ω1..ω6`), `L5` = Player B Boss (`Ψ`), `L6–L8` = Player B Minions (`ψ1..ψ6`). Right = same as Single-Player PvE.
    - *2-Player Versus*: Left `L1` = Player A Boss (`Ω`), `L2–L4` = Player A Minions (`ω1..ω6`), `L5` empty, `L6–L8` = 6 Target Pucks (TBC). Right `R1` = Player B Boss (`Ψ`), `R2–R4` = Player B Minions (`ψ1..ψ6`), `R5` empty, `R6–R8` = 6 Target Pucks (TBC).
  - **Adventurer Slot Mapping (`1..6` -> `V, B, R, S, F, E` / `v, b, r, s, f, e`)**:
    - In normal random Adventurer Team generation: `1` (`V`/`v` at `R21`/`R61`) = Knight, `2` (`B`/`b` at `R22`/`R62`) = Warrior, `3` (`R`/`r` at `R31`/`R71`) = Ranger, `4` (`S`/`s` at `R32`/`R72`) = Mage, `5` (`F`/`f` at `R41`/`R81`) and `6` (`E`/`e` at `R42`/`R82`) = free positions for duplicate classes. If a class is absent in a small team, its position and grid cell remain empty (never compacted). Customized stage team definitions may explicitly assign any class to any slot `1..6`.
  - **Colour Palette & Puck Stripes**:
    - Enemy Adventurer Puck & Unit Display Border by **Class**: **Knight = Blue**, **Warrior = Red**, **Ranger = Green**, **Mage = Purple**.
    - Minion Puck & Unit Display Border by slot index `1..6`: deeper shade of the `1..6` colour palette.
    - Boss Monster Puck & Unit Display Border: Black.
    - Reference Mark text/symbol colour: Player A (`Ω`, `ω1..6`) = Gold; Player B (`Ψ`, `ψ1..6`) = Silver; Adventurer Team 1 (`V, B, R, S, F, E`) = Deep Grey; Adventurer Team 2 (`v, b, r, s, f, e`) = Deep Brown.
    - Puck Stripes:
      - *Team Stripe*: Team 1 = none; Team 2 = thin **White** line stripe on 2 opposite sides of the puck.
      - *Free-Position Stripe*: Slots `1..4` = none; Slot `5` (`F`/`f`) = thin **White** 4-sided X stripe; Slot `6` (`E`/`e`) = thin **Black** 4-sided X stripe.
- **Damage Calculation & Settle Pipeline**:
  - **Frenzy Power**: Held by Boss Monsters only. Starts at `1.0` (`100%`), minimum floor `0.5` (`50%`). On Boss Monster puck collision with a non-Knight Enemy Adventurer puck: `+0.10` (`+10%`). On Boss Monster puck collision with a Knight puck: `-0.20` (`-20%`), clamped to `>= 0.50`. Special blocks/effects can also adjust it.
  - **Hit Level**: Held by all units. Integer defaulting to `0`, modified by conditional effects (hitting a Focusing puck, special cushions, type effectiveness). Multiplier is $1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}$.
  - **Pre-Settle Ghost HP (HP Trailing)**:
    - Computed whenever `damage_stack` or `hit_level` changes during `Puck-puck Time!` or `Action Time`:
      $$\text{Preview Damage} = \text{round}\left(\left(\sum_{d \in \text{Damage Stack}} d.\text{value}\right) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}\right)$$
    - `ghost_hp = max(0, current_hp - preview_damage)`. Does **not** include `Frenzy Power`.
  - **Settle Time Resolution**:
    - For an **Enemy Adventurer**:
      $$\text{Settled Damage} = \text{round}\left(\left(\sum_{d \in \text{Damage Stack}} d.\text{value} \times \text{Frenzy}(d.\text{owner\_boss\_unit\_id})\right) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}\right)$$
    - For a **Boss Monster or Minion**:
      $$\text{Settled Damage} = \text{round}\left(\left(\sum_{d \in \text{Damage Stack}} d.\text{value}\right) \times 1.1^{\text{Hit Level}}\right)$$
    - **Boss Monster 1-Bar Cap**: If `Settled Damage >= current_hp` on a Boss Monster, the current Health Bar clears (`current_hp` drops to `0`, triggering bar break: if `bars_remaining > 0`, `bars_remaining` decrements by `1` and `current_hp` resets to `max_hp` with all excess damage discarded; if `bars_remaining == 0`, the Boss Monster is defeated). A Boss Monster never loses more than 1 Health Bar in a single Settle Phase.
    - **Post-Settle Reset & Decay**: Clear `damage_stack`, reset `hit_level = 0` on all units, and update every Boss Monster's Frenzy Power to `new_frenzy = (current_frenzy / 2.0) + 0.50` (i.e. `current / 2 + 50%`).
- **`UnitInformationBoard`, `UnitDisplay` & `UnitDetailDisplay` (View)**:
  - Replaces the old `_enemy_bars` 3D-to-2D floating health bars and placeholder `_unit_side_cards` in the battlefield canvas.
  - Each `UnitDisplay` renders: portrait, Reference Mark (upper-left), Class Mark (upper-right, Adventurers only), Action Tag countdown integer(s) (right/lower-right of portrait; or pocket return countdown if `is_pocketed`), HP bar + Ghost HP bar (bottom), and Damage Stack count circle badge (left of HP bar, hidden when count is `0`, shaking on new damage).
  - Boss Monster 2-cell `UnitDisplay`: displays Frenzy Power percentage on the right side. Omits its internal HP bar if and only if that Boss Monster belongs to the local seated player shown in the upper-left HUD (`Player A` for Player A and Spectators; `Player B` for Player B); all other Boss Monsters render their HP bar and `x N` bar count inside their 2-cell `UnitDisplay`.
  - Hovering a `UnitDisplay` sets a highlight state on all active `pucks` in its `UnitInformationDO`.
  - Clicking a `UnitDisplay` opens `UnitDetailDisplay` showing localized fields: Unit Name, Class Name & Class Mark icon (if Adventurer), HP Bar with numeric `HP / Max HP` (and bar count if Boss), Attack Power, Mass & Radius, Skills, Special Condition, Statuses (buffs/debuffs), Hit Level, and Frenzy Power. Clicking outside or re-clicking the same cell closes `UnitDetailDisplay`.
  - All user-facing labels go through the multi-language localization holder.

## Testing Decisions

- **What makes a good test**: Drive only the public seams (`UnitInformationManager`'s public API/signals and `UnitInformationBoard`'s public view/interaction methods) and assert externally observable domain state and rendered view state — never internal private variables, tween objects, or node tree layout constants.
- **Two seams (following the `time-board-display` model/view precedent)**:
  1. **The `UnitInformationManager` Seam (Pure Model & Damage Calculation)**:
     - Register Boss Monsters, Minions, and Enemy Adventurers across Single-Player PvE, 2-Player Co-op, and 2-Player Versus configurations.
     - Assert Reference Mark generation (`Ω`, `ω1..6`, `Ψ`, `ψ1..6`, `A1..A6`, `B1..B6`), fixed grid coordinates (`{L|R}{1..8}{1..2}`), skipped-class empty slots (e.g. missing Warrior leaves `?2` / `R22` empty without compacting), custom stage overrides, and slot/reference-mark colour assignments.
     - Push `DamageDO` entries onto units' `Damage Stack`s, adjust `Hit Level` and Boss `Frenzy Power` (including `+10%` on non-Knight hit, `-20%` on Knight hit, `50%` floor), and assert pre-settle Ghost HP (`sum * 1.1^hit_level`, excluding Frenzy Power) vs. settled damage at `Settle Time` (including per-`DamageDO` source Boss Frenzy Power in 1P and 2P Co-op).
     - Assert the Boss Monster 1-Health-Bar-per-Settle cap (excess damage discarded on bar break), post-Settle `Damage Stack` clear, `Hit Level` reset to `0`, and `Frenzy Power` decay (`current / 2 + 50%` for values both above and below `100%`).
     - Assert multi-puck registration (`0..N` pucks per unit) routing damage to the same unit.
     - Assert that `PuckVelocityManager.resolve_puck_puck_collision(p1, p2)` calculates collision damage linearly from the Impulse scalar `J` (~`50..200` range), respects the Knight collision matrix, adjusts Boss Monster `Frenzy Power`, and pushes `DamageDO` records into `UnitInformationManager`, and that `current_hp` / `max_hp` (with Adventurer HP scaled to **Mage = 1500**, **Warrior = 2200**, **Ranger = 2200**, **Knight = 3000**) live in `UnitInformationDO` rather than on the puck physics scripts.
  2. **The `UnitInformationBoard` / `UnitDetailDisplay` View Seam**:
     - Feed manager state into the board and assert cell occupancy at `{L|R}{1..8}{1..2}`, Boss 2-cell span Frenzy Power display, conditional Boss HP bar visibility based on local player seat (`Player A`, `Player B`, `Spectator`), Damage Stack badge visibility (`0` hidden, `>0` visible with count), Ghost HP bar ratio, Defeated state (grey + red cross + hidden HP/turns), and Pocketed state (grey + visible HP + return turn countdown).
     - Simulate mouse hover on a `UnitDisplay` and assert all `0..N` associated pucks enter their highlighted state; simulate click on a `UnitDisplay` to assert `UnitDetailDisplay` opens with the unit's numeric `HP / Max HP`, power, mass, radius, class, skills, and statuses, and closes on outside click.
- **Prior art**: `tests/test_time_board_model.gd` and `tests/test_time_board_view.gd` (model/view split tested headless via `tests/_run_all.gd`), `tests/test_timing_manager.gd`, and `tests/test_battlefield_data_sync.gd`.

## Out of Scope

- Concrete skill pool designs, buff/debuff gameplay mechanics, and Type Effectiveness definitions (covered by `enemy-adventurers` and future skill/status specs; the UI and DO provide the hooks and display containers).
- Target Puck gameplay rules in 2-Player Versus mode (rows `L6–L8` and `R6–R8` are reserved in the board layout, while Target Puck combat logic remains TBC).
- Special table blocks or special cushion blocks that modify Frenzy Power or Hit Level (covered by future cue-table block specs; `UnitInformationManager` exposes the `modify_frenzy_power()` and `modify_hit_level()` APIs for them).
- Final artistic portraits, custom shader artwork for the Frenzy Power spread animation, and final icon textures (placeholder/procedural visuals are used initially and verified via human UI review per `AGENTS.md`).
